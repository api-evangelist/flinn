# Flinn

> Flinn (flinn.ai) is an AI-powered regulatory and quality platform for medical technology and in-vitro diagnostics (IVD) manufacturers. It automates post-market surveillance (PMS) and compliance across the EU MDR, IVDR, and FDA 21 CFR frameworks — monitoring 15+ safety databases, running AI-assisted literature evaluation, automating complaint handling, and producing audit-ready reports. Used by 200+ global MedTech and IVD organizations. Backed by HV Capital and Speedinvest.
